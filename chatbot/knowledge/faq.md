# AnnaSetu — FAQ (Fact-Checked)

This file is the ONLY source the chatbot uses to answer legal, food-safety,
and policy questions. Every answer has been fact-checked by the team.
If a question is not answered here, the bot says:
"I don't know; please contact [team number]."

The bot NEVER gives legal advice beyond what is written here.

---

## Q: Is my food donation legally protected?

Yes. Under the **FSSAI (Recovery of Surplus Food) Regulations**, a donor who
donates food in good faith to an NGO or registered recipient is protected from
liability, provided the food was fit for consumption at the time of donation.
This applies to restaurants, caterers, wedding halls, and mess operators.

*Source: FSSAI Recovery of Surplus Food Regulations (Draft, notified 2019).*

---

## Q: What does "safe until" mean?

"Safe until" is the time you estimate the food can be safely eaten.
AnnaSetu uses this time to:
- Only route your donation to shelters whose driver can arrive **15 minutes before** this time.
- Automatically stop matching and move to Rescue Deals or diversion if the window closes.

**You are responsible for setting an accurate safe-until time.**
AnnaSetu never overrides or extends it.

---

## Q: Who is responsible for food safety?

The **donor** is responsible for ensuring the food is fit for consumption
at the time of donation. AnnaSetu's role is logistics only — we match and
move food; we do not inspect, test, or certify it.

## Q: Is there a cost for using AnnaSetu?

- **Donors, shelters, drivers**: free, always.
- **Rescue Deal buyers**: pay the donor directly at pickup (cash or UPI, no in-app payment).
- **CSR/ESG reports**: free for the MVP; paid tier is a roadmap item for businesses.

## Q: How does the OTP work?

- When a donation is matched, the donor receives a **Pickup OTP** to share with the driver.
- When the driver arrives at the shelter, the shelter coordinator gives the driver a **Delivery OTP** (received when they accepted the offer).
- The driver enters both OTPs to confirm pickup and delivery.
- OTPs are 4–6 digits and expire after delivery is complete.

---

## Q: What happens if no shelter accepts?

If all shelters time out or decline, the donation becomes **UNMATCHED**
and the system automatically tries:
1. **Rescue Deals** — listed at ≤ ₹30/meal for nearby buyers (if food is still safe for 90+ minutes and the donor has an FSSAI licence).
2. **Non-human diversion** — offered to animal feeders (gaushalas) or compost/biogas units so nothing reaches landfill.
3. If none of these succeed: the donation is marked **EXPIRED**.

## Q: How do I update my shelter's capacity?

Send a WhatsApp message like:
- "capacity 40" — sets available meals to 40
- "aaj 40 le sakte hain" — same in Hinglish

You can update capacity at any time; it takes effect immediately.

## Q: How do I opt out?

Reply **STOP** to any message from AnnaSetu to opt out of WhatsApp notifications.
Your data (phone number and session) is deleted within 24 hours.
To re-enable, send any message to the AnnaSetu number.

## Q: Is my data private?

- Phone numbers are stored only for coordination purposes.
- Chat sessions have a **24-hour TTL** and are deleted automatically.
- No chat content appears in CSR/ESG reports — only aggregate impact numbers.
- We do not share your data with third parties.

## Q: What food categories does AnnaSetu accept?

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Cooked   | Dal-chawal, biryani, roti-sabzi, curry |
| Raw      | Vegetables, fruits, uncooked grains |
| Packaged | Sealed packaged food within expiry |
| Bakery   | Bread, cakes, pastries |

Temperature-sensitive items (e.g., ice cream) are not eligible for Rescue Deals
but can go to diversion partners.

---

## Q: What is a Rescue Deal?

A **Rescue Deal** is surplus food listed at a nominal price (≤ ₹30/meal)
for pickup by nearby buyers — students, workers, residents.

- The food is sold **directly by the donor**; AnnaSetu is only the listing platform.
- There are **no in-app payments**; buyers pay by cash or UPI at pickup.
- Every deal screen shows: *"Surplus food sold directly by [donor]. Consume before [time]."*
- Rescue Deals are only available after Tier 1 shelter matching has run and failed.
